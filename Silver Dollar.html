Care Notes
Silver Dollars are large, fast-swimming schooling tetras that need a spacious tank with strong filtration and open swimming room. They're skittish alone or in small numbers — always keep in groups of 5+ to reduce stress and stress-related jumping/darting. They're near-100% herbivorous and will demolish live plants, so use plastic/silk decor or plant-safe alternatives like Anubias and Java Fern only as an occasional test (results vary).
