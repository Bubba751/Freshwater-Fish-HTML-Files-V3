Cichlid Arowana (Small)

Osteoglossum bicirrhosum
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Aggressive/predatory as it matures
Grows To
24-36"+ (very large adult)
Starting Tank Size
55 Gallon
Typical Color
Silver with iridescent scales
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Omnivore
Frozen Bloodworms
Frozen Brine
Hikari Vibra Bites
Sera Flakes
Hikari Cichlid Pellets
Care Notes
Grows extremely large; needs a tight-fitting lid (jumper) and eventually a very large tank; predatory diet
Temp Range
75-82°F
Water PH
6.0-6.5
Water KH
1-5 dKH
Origin
Amazon basin, South America
Plant Safety
Mostly safe (large adults may uproot)
