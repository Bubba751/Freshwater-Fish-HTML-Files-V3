Care Notes
Blue Acaras are a hardy, adaptable, and relatively peaceful cichlid — a great "gateway" cichlid for hobbyists moving up from community tanks. They tolerate a wide pH/temperature range and are far more forgiving of water parameters than many other cichlids. Provide sand or fine gravel substrate (they like to sift), rockwork, and driftwood with some open swimming space. They'll dig moderately but are not typically destructive to well-anchored plants — Anubias, Java Fern, and Vallisneria all do well with them
